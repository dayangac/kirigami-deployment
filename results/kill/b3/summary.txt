------------------------------------------------------- the periodic identity, repaired
B3 section 5 asserts  sum_split r_e = det(P_0) tr K - W  with W the per-cell hinge sum.
That is FALSE as literally stated.  Both copies of an edge contribute
<x_a - x_b, u_f1 - u_f0>; for a SPLIT edge sigma_f0 = sigma_f1 so the term is invariant
under a lattice translation, but for a HINGE edge sigma_f0 = -sigma_f1 and
u_{f1+t} - u_{f0+t} = u_f1 - u_f0 + sigma_f1 t, so the term depends on WHICH LIFT of the
edge the preimage walk uses.  Summing one arbitrary representative per quotient edge
misses a defect measured at 0.38 - 1.88 x det P_0 on the 33 K7 patterns -- comparable to
the budget itself.  Summing instead over one representative PREIMAGE per translation
class, each at its own lift (method::periodic_cell_edges), restores
        W + R = det(P_0) tr K
to 1e-15 on 30 of the 33 patterns and on 46 of the 50 C3 rows.  The 4 exceptions are
squares_3x2 (the 3x3 super patch does not carry a full representative set: 8 of 10 hinge
and 0 of 2 split classes covered) and snub_square_3x3 (M' has 9 components, so u has 9
independent BFS roots and K is gauge dependent -- already recorded in STATE.md F33).
Doctests: 5 new TEST_CASEs in code/tests/test_method.cpp cover the potential closure,
q_e = 2 r_e exactly, the patch identity, the measured void harmonic and the periodic
identity.  Full suite 80 cases / 16,785 assertions pass.

CONSEQUENCE FOR B3.  With the identity repaired,  tr K - tau* = R / det(P_0)  EXACTLY.
The "budget gap" is the total split budget rescaled.  So (i) the theorem's implication
tr K < tau* => min_e q_e < 0 is true but is the averaging bound min_e r_e <= R/n_split
rewritten, and (ii) tau* is a function of the design, not of the pattern, so the
constrained problem is not a linear program on the achievable set.

------------------------------------------------ stage 2: constrained re-solve (bar 2)
b3_constrained.csv, 50 rows x 3 margins.  Algorithm: minimise ||K - K*|| over the
achievable set subject to tr K >= tau* + delta |tau*|.  Because g = A^T vec(I), the
minimiser is K* + ((tau*(1+delta) - tr K*) / 2) I when dim K = 4 -- an isotropic
expansion added to the target -- and the leftover freedom is the SAME null space the
baseline searched, so it is spent on the 0+ margin exactly as C3 did.  tau* is refreshed
by a 4-step fixed point because it depends on the design.
  delta   certified            min q > 0        max ||K - K*||
  0.0     12 -> 13  (+1, -0)   21 -> 37         10.4
  0.1     12 -> 14  (+2, -0)   21 -> 37         19.8
  0.5     12 ->  2  (+1, -11)  21 -> 26        588.9
Bar 2 (beat 12/50 by a margin surviving the 50-row binomial error bar): FAIL.  +1 and +2
of 50 are inside +-4.6 (1 sigma at p = 0.24, n = 50).  The constraint DOES do what the
theorem says -- it nearly doubles the number of designs whose split cuts all open at 0+,
21 -> 37 -- but the extra 0+ feasibility does not convert into certified deployment
range, which is the K5/K6/F30 obstruction again and is not a budget phenomenon.  And the
price is that the target is abandoned: the isotropic correction is 10 to 589 in Frobenius
norm, so the "designed" Jacobian is not the requested one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------- CONTRADICTIONS
1. B3 section 5's identity, as written, is wrong (the lift defect above).  The repaired
   identity holds, and B1's (B.5) coefficients are confirmed independently: on 33
   patterns W + R equals det P_0 tr K, which K7 C4 had already matched to the measured
   per-cell void harmonic.
2. B3 section 2's "the design variable K enters the feasibility problem only through the
   linear functional tr K" is not supported: tr K - tau* = R / det P_0, and R is a
   quadratic function of the design at fixed tr K.  A pattern can move R without moving
   tr K, which is exactly what the baseline free-subspace search already does.
3. Nothing here contradicts STATE.md.  F33's C3 12/25 (= 12/50 rows) is reproduced bit
   for bit with the repaired certificate, and F33's snub_square_3x3 gauge caveat is the
