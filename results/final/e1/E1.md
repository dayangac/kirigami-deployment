# E1 -- exact Theta_max (T4.2") vs. bisection referee, extended population

Extends K2a (results/kill/KILL_REPORT.md sec. K2a; 187 tightly-embedded designs, all
authored-tiling-derived) to a 3113-design population that spans random Voronoi/
Delaunay/quad-random graphs and both orientation rules (sigma_mc = Eq. (1) relaxation,
sigma_def = K5's deployability-defect greedy search), using the F34-fixed,
tolerance-independent referee (results/core_validation/referee_fix.md; a single
tol_rel = 1e-12 is reported, since referee_fix.md measured it agrees with 1e-9 and 1e-6
on K2a's population).

Code: `Kirigami/apps/kill_e1.jl` (new; does not modify any core/ or method/ source). Three
populations, all filtered to "tightly embedded" exactly as K2a's own
`deployable_population` does (uniformly deployable, no inverted face, no collision at
theta = 0, tol_rel = 1e-12):

- **authored**: `kill_common.jl::deployable_population()` (frozen in `data/corpus/`) called with (samples_per_base=20,
  radius_frac=0.2) and again with (20, 0.35) -- more seeds/samples than K2a's (8, 0.2) --
  over the same 7 authored families x 5 clip radii. sigma_mc is whatever the function
  assigns (checkerboard or Eq. (1) relaxation); a sigma_def row is added per design by
  K5's greedy defect search at the same X, re-solved and re-filtered.
- **random**: `kill_common.jl::make_graph(id, 100, 800, 1400)` (frozen in `data/corpus/`) -- Voronoi / Delaunay /
  quad-random, K5's exact population call (measured there to give sigma_def a 61.5%
  embedding rate under the *withdrawn* theta=0 predicate; under this run's tighter,
  uniformly-deployable-AND-embedded filter the yield is lower, see below), ids 0-899,
  both sigma rules.
- **jitter**: the 4 split-bearing authored tilings only (hexagons_auto,
  truncated_square_488, snub_square_33434, tiling_3_4_3_12 -- the other 4 authored
  tilings are split-free, STATE.md F30/A3), interior-vertex Gaussian jitter at 12
  log-spaced amplitudes in [0.01, 0.6] median edge lengths (below/around the known
  a* = 0.28/0.32/0.16/0.52) x 15 seeds x 2 sigma rules, plus the a=0 baseline.

Sharded 12x (`xargs -P 12`, `code/scripts/run_e1.sh`); shards merged into
`results/final/e1/e1.csv`.

## Headline

- **N designs: 3113**
- **Agreement at 1e-5** (`|Theta_max(T4.2") - bisect| <= 1e-5`): **3113 / 3113 (100.00 %)**
- **Worst gap: 3.822e-09** (all gaps are floating-point-scale, not resolution-limited --
  the referee's 4000-point grid + 50 bisection steps at tol_rel = 1e-12 tracks the exact
  T4.2" interval scan everywhere in this population)

## Certificate soundness (holds => Theta_max >= eps, eps = 0.006, K2a's value)

- Certificate holds on **2150 / 3113** designs.
- **Soundness: 0 / 2150 violations** -- every design the certificate calls valid the
  referee confirms has `Theta_max >= eps`. Matches K2a's and A3's post-F32 finding of 0
  violations, now over 3113 designs instead of 187 (K2a) or the jitter ladder's 6416
  rows (A3).

## Certificate exactness

- Certified **2150** vs. actually `Theta_max >= eps` **2150 / 3113** -- **exact on this
  population**: every design with `Theta_max >= 0.006` is certified, and every certified
  design has `Theta_max >= 0.006`. Consistent with F32's finding that `NOROOT(eps)` is
  exact for `eps` below the first graze and this population's eps (0.006) sits below
  every graze angle measured so far.

All 13 families agree at 1e-5 on every design. Only the 3 random-graph families never
certify or reach `Theta_max >= eps`: **random sigma_mc gives 0 tightly-embedded designs
out of 900 ids tried** (consistent with K1a's 0/200 injective under Eq. (1) orientation);
random sigma_def (K5's defect search) gives 131 tightly-embedded designs (58 Delaunay, 59
quad-random, 14 Voronoi) -- lower than K5's own 61.5% because this run's filter is
strictly tighter (uniformly deployable AND collision-free at theta=0, vs. K5's
theta=0-collision-only predicate, and K5's predicate has since been shown unsound by
F34) -- and every one of those 131 has `Theta_max = 0` under the exact scan (the 0+
split-duplicate collision K5/K6 already found on random graphs), so none certify.

## Wall time

12-way sharded (`xargs -P 12`, `code/scripts/run_e1.sh`): slowest shard 311.1 s; sum of
per-design measurement time (exact T4.2" scan + referee bisection + certificate) across
all 3113 rows, 352.1 s. Sequential single-shard smoke test (900-id-equivalent random
population omitted, 60 ids) took 298.9 s for 3020 designs, confirming the random
population (Voronoi/Delaunay/quad, defect search) is the wall-time bottleneck per design,
not the authored/jitter population.
