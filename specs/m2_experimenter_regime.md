## Why this exists
REPORT.md §"What a reviewer will object to", items 2 and 4: the random population has median `|F| = 332.5`, range [101, 793], while every example in the 2026 paper has `F ∈ [4, 97]`; and the paper never claims random graphs. A referee will ask (a) whether the paper's own projection also fails at the paper's own scale, and whether our method works there, and (b) how the exact range, certificate and design routine scale — MISSION.md §3 demanded 100–5,000 faces and nothing above 800 has ever been run.
